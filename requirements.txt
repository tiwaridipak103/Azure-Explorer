household_total_liabilities can have a very meaningful but delayed relationship with real estate vacancy, especially in commercial real estate (CRE). Since your data is quarterly and multi-market, this feature is often acting as a macro-financial stress + consumption capacity proxy.

What is household_total_liabilities?
It usually includes:

Mortgage debt

Credit card debt

Auto loans

Student loans

Personal loans

HELOCs

So this variable measures:

“How leveraged households are in a market.”

Higher liabilities = households are carrying more debt obligations.

Why would household liabilities affect CRE vacancy?
Because household financial health indirectly drives:

consumer spending

office demand

apartment affordability

retail activity

migration

local economic resilience

The effect differs by property type.

Property-Type Interpretation
1. Apartments / Multifamily
This is usually the strongest connection.

Mechanism
When household liabilities rise:

debt servicing burden rises

affordability weakens

defaults/delinquency risk increases

renters double-up

household formation slows

This can eventually increase apartment vacancy.

Typical Chain
Higher household liabilities
→ lower disposable income
→ weaker rent affordability
→ slower leasing
→ rising concessions
→ higher vacancy
But there is a twist.

Important Nonlinear Effect
At moderate levels:

Higher liabilities may actually signal:

strong housing demand

active mortgage markets

growing economies

expanding credit access

which can REDUCE vacancy initially.

But beyond a threshold:

Excess leverage
→ financial stress
→ demand destruction
→ vacancy increase
So relationship is often:

U-shaped or regime-based
Very common in CRE.

Office Vacancy Relationship
More indirect.

Household leverage affects:

local consumption

business formation

labor market health

If households become financially stressed:

companies slow hiring

layoffs increase

office absorption weakens

Then office vacancy rises with lag.

Retail Vacancy Relationship
This is usually highly important.

Retail depends heavily on:

Consumer spending power
If household liabilities increase too aggressively:

Higher debt payments
→ lower discretionary spending
→ weaker retailer sales
→ store closures
→ higher retail vacancy
Retail often reacts faster than office.

Industrial Vacancy Relationship
Weakest direct relationship.

But there is still a channel:

Consumer debt stress
→ lower goods consumption
→ weaker logistics demand
→ slower warehouse absorption
Usually weaker but still meaningful.

Why This Feature Often Has Lagged Effects
Debt stress does NOT impact vacancy immediately.

CRE markets move slowly.

Typical delay:

2 quarters

4 quarters

sometimes 6+ quarters

because leases are sticky.

So in your quarterly panel dataset:

Lag features are extremely important.
