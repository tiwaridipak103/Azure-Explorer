Below are more advanced and realistic feature engineering ideas specifically for quarterly CRE vacancy modeling.

1. Monetary Tightening Velocity
Not the level — the speed of tightening.

fedfunds_velocity_2q = (
    federal_funds_rate -
    federal_funds_rate.shift(2)
) / 2
Why it matters:

CRE breaks during rapid repricing cycles

sudden financing shock hurts transactions/leasing

Example:

5% stable = manageable

0% → 5% rapidly = dangerous

Very institutional-grade feature.

2. Policy Acceleration Feature
Second derivative of rates.

fedfunds_acceleration = (
    fedfunds_velocity_2q -
    fedfunds_velocity_2q.shift(1)
)
Captures:

whether tightening is speeding up

panic phase of monetary cycle

Very useful around:

recession transitions

banking stress periods

3. Rate Shock Persistence
Measures how long restrictive policy persists.

high_rate_flag = (federal_funds_rate > 4).astype(int)

tightening_persistence = (
    high_rate_flag
    .rolling(4)
    .sum()
)
Why important:
CRE pain increases when high rates remain elevated for many quarters.

Refinancing wall pressure accumulates slowly.

4. Refinancing Stress Proxy
Extremely relevant for CRE.

refi_stress = (
    federal_funds_rate *
    commercial_total_liabilities
)
Markets with:

large debt loads

high floating-rate exposure

experience major vacancy pressure later.

Especially powerful for:

office

transitional assets

5. Debt-Service Shock
Approximate financing burden increase.

debt_service_shock = (
    federal_funds_rate.diff(4) *
    commercial_total_liabilities
)
Captures:

refinancing repricing risk

debt rollover pressure

This is closer to how REIT analysts think.

6. Capital Freeze Indicator
CRE transactions freeze during aggressive tightening.

capital_freeze = (
    (federal_funds_rate > 4) &
    (fedfunds_4q_change > 1.5)
).astype(int)
Why useful:
During freeze periods:

deals collapse

leasing weakens

construction halts

distress rises

Vacancy often rises with lag.

7. Supply Suppression Signal
High rates kill future supply.

supply_suppression = (
    federal_funds_rate *
    building_permits.diff(2)
)
Counterintuitive effect:

high rates today

lower construction tomorrow

lower future vacancy later

Very important for:

multifamily

industrial

8. Asymmetric Rate Impact
Markets react differently to hikes vs cuts.

Create separate features.

rate_hikes_only = fedfunds_4q_change.clip(lower=0)

rate_cuts_only = (
    fedfunds_4q_change.clip(upper=0)
)
Why?
CRE responds asymmetrically:

hikes hurt quickly

cuts help slowly

Institutional macro models often separate these.

9. Restrictive Monetary Regime Duration
restrictive_regime = (
    federal_funds_rate > inflation_rate
).astype(int)

restrictive_duration = (
    restrictive_regime
    .groupby(market)
    .cumsum()
)
This approximates:
real restrictive policy duration.

Long restrictive periods:

reduce demand

increase defaults

pressure occupancy

10. Market Sensitivity Beta

market_rate_beta =

Very advanced panel feature.
