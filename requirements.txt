Secured_Overnight_Financing_Rate (SOFR) is one of the most important macro-financial variables for commercial real estate modeling, especially for vacancy forecasting.

You can think of it as the base short-term cost of money in the US financial system.

Secured Overnight Financing Rate

In real estate, SOFR affects:

borrowing cost

refinancing risk

cap rates

transaction activity

construction financing

tenant expansion behavior

ultimately vacancy

Intuition First
When SOFR rises:

loans become expensive

floating-rate debt payments jump

developers slow construction

companies reduce expansion

leasing activity weakens

distressed properties increase

This usually increases vacancy after some lag.

When SOFR falls:

financing becomes cheaper

investment activity rises

tenants expand

absorption improves

Vacancy often declines later.

Why SOFR Matters More After 2021
Historically many CRE loans used LIBOR.

After LIBOR transition, SOFR became the dominant benchmark for:

bridge loans

floating-rate CRE debt

construction loans

CMBS floating structures

syndicated real estate financing

So your dataset likely captures:

monetary tightening

liquidity stress

refinancing pressure

through SOFR.

How SOFR Impacts Different Property Types
Apartments
Higher SOFR:

multifamily acquisitions slow

refinancing stress increases

new construction slows

renters may stay longer (positive)

but economic slowdown hurts household formation

Net effect:

short-term stable

medium-term vacancy can rise

Office
Office is highly SOFR-sensitive because:

office values depend heavily on cap rates

refinancing risk is huge

tenants cut expansion during high-rate periods

Typical pattern:

SOFR ↑ → office leasing ↓ → vacancy ↑

This became extremely visible after 2022.

Industrial
Industrial is less rate-sensitive operationally, but:

e-commerce expansion slows

warehouse construction financing weakens

Usually moderate delayed effect.

Retail
Retail reacts indirectly:

consumers spend less

retailers close weak stores

expansion pauses

Vacancy rises mainly during prolonged high-rate regimes.

Core Economic Transmission Mechanism
The chain usually looks like:

SOFR ↑
→ Treasury yields ↑
→ CRE loan coupons ↑
→ Debt service burden ↑
→ Cap rates ↑
→ Property values ↓
→ Transaction volume ↓
→ Construction slowdown
→ Tenant expansion slows
→ Vacancy ↑

Important Timing Insight
SOFR rarely impacts vacancy immediately.

Real estate reacts slowly.

Typical lag:

2 to 8 quarters

Because:

leases are sticky

projects take time

refinancing cycles are delayed

This is extremely important for feature engineering.

What You Should Expect in Your Data
During Rapid SOFR Increases
Example:
2022–2023 tightening cycle.

You may observe:

office vacancy surge

transaction freeze

lower absorption

refinancing stress

But:
vacancy may rise with delay.

So contemporaneous correlation may look weak.

Lagged correlation is often stronger.

What Sophisticated CRE Models Usually Do
Top institutional real estate models rarely use raw SOFR directly.

Instead they engineer:

1. SOFR Regime
Example:

low-rate regime

neutral regime

restrictive regime

Because markets behave differently under each regime.

2. Rate Shock
Quarter-over-quarter change.

Reason:
markets react more to surprises than absolute levels.

Example:

SOFR rising from 0.25% → 4% rapidly
creates panic.

3. Debt Stress Indicator
SOFR combined with:

cap rate

NOI growth

debt coverage

This captures refinancing pressure.

4. Lagged SOFR
Very important.

Examples:

SOFR lag 2Q

SOFR lag 4Q

distributed lag averages

Because vacancy reacts slowly.

5. Yield Curve Relationships
SOFR relative to:

10Y Treasury

mortgage spread

credit spread

Institutional investors care about spreads more than standalone rates.

How to Interpret Model Coefficient
Suppose your model says:

SOFR coefficient = +0.8

Interpretation:

Holding everything else constant,
a rise in SOFR is associated with higher vacancy.

But interpretation depends on:

property type

market

lag structure

regime

Why Relationship Can Sometimes Look Opposite
Very important in CRE.

Sometimes:
SOFR ↑ while vacancy ↓

Why?

Because:

economy may still be strong

employment booming

tenants expanding despite higher rates

This happened early in tightening cycles.

So SOFR often acts as:

a delayed stress variable
rather than immediate recession variable.

Real Estate Industry Insight
Institutional CRE investors often monitor:

“higher for longer” rate environment

refinancing wall

floating-rate exposure

debt maturity schedules

All of these are directly connected to SOFR.

That is why SOFR became one of the most important variables in post-2021 CRE analytics.

lagged SOFR

SOFR change

rolling average SOFR

interaction with property type

interaction with leverage-sensitive markets

because raw contemporaneous SOFR alone often underperforms.
