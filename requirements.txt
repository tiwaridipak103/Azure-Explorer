6. Why raw retail sales can mislead
This is where many models fail.

Raw retail sales contain:

(a) Inflation
Sales may rise because prices rose, not demand.

Example:

Units sold unchanged

Prices +8%

Sales increase artificially.

Model may falsely infer stronger occupancy demand.

(b) Seasonality
Retail spikes every holiday season.

Q4 sales jump naturally.

Without adjustment:

model learns seasonal noise.

(c) National vs local mismatch
If this is national retail sales but your vacancy is market-level:

You’re assuming national consumer behavior affects all metros equally.

That’s often false.

Atlanta and New York react differently.
