Household_Net_Worth is actually a very powerful macroeconomic variable for explaining real estate vacancy because it captures the financial strength of households in a market.

In CRE and housing economics, vacancy is not only driven by supply — it is heavily driven by the ability and confidence of people to spend, rent, buy, upgrade, and expand businesses. Household net worth becomes a proxy for that strength.

But the relationship differs by property type.

Mechanism by Property Type
1. Apartment Vacancy
This is often the strongest relationship.

When net worth rises:

renters move out of shared housing

more people can afford independent units

migration into premium apartments rises

delayed household formation reverses

Result:

apartment demand increases

occupancy improves

vacancy declines

But there is a twist:

Very high net worth growth can ALSO increase:

home purchases

single-family ownership migration

That can sometimes increase apartment vacancy in affluent suburban markets.

So relationship may become nonlinear.

2. Retail Vacancy
Household net worth strongly affects retail through the wealth effect.

When people feel wealthier:

discretionary spending rises

luxury spending rises

restaurant traffic rises

retail sales improve

Retailers then:

expand stores

keep weak stores alive longer

lease more space

Vacancy falls.

This is especially true in:

lifestyle centers

affluent metros

luxury retail corridors

3. Industrial Vacancy
The relationship is indirect.

Higher household wealth →
higher consumption →
higher e-commerce demand →
higher warehousing demand.

Especially important after:

stock market booms

housing appreciation cycles

Industrial demand tends to lag household wealth changes by several quarters.

4. Office Vacancy
Office relationship is weakest and delayed.

Why?

Household net worth affects:

business confidence

hiring

professional services expansion

But office demand depends more on:

employment growth

corporate profitability

hybrid work adoption

So household wealth only indirectly matters.

Timing Matters (Very Important)
Net worth affects vacancy with lag.

Households do not instantly:

Nonlinear Relationship
This variable rarely behaves linearly.

Typical pattern:

Net Worth State	Vacancy Impact
Weak growth	little demand
Moderate growth	strong occupancy improvement
Extremely high growth	ownership substitution may appear
So tree models often capture this better than linear models.

Very Important Real Estate Interpretation

Household net worth measures how financially comfortable households feel.

Financial comfort drives space consumption.

That is the economic bridge between the two variables.
