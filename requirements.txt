Since your dataset is quarterly and multi-market, this variable can capture business cycle strength and tenant expansion behavior across markets.

1. What is Corporate_Profits_After_Tax?
It measures:

Total profits companies earn after paying taxes.

Higher profits mean:

companies are financially healthy

firms expand operations

businesses hire more employees

firms lease more office/industrial space

retailers open stores

logistics firms expand warehouses

Lower profits mean:

cost cutting

layoffs

downsizing

delayed expansion

office consolidation

warehouse reduction

So this feature acts as a business expansion/contraction signal.

Usually:

Higher Corporate Profits → Lower Vacancy
